Site theme

Use

Load the shared foundation before a site-specific stylesheet:

<script src="https://jehlp.net/site-theme/v2/theme.js"></script>
<link rel="stylesheet" href="https://jehlp.net/site-theme/v2/base.css">
<link rel="stylesheet" href="assets/styles.css">

Readable at every density

Keep reading at 16px or larger, regular controls at 14px, and secondary metadata at 12px. Wrap a toolbar before shrinking its labels. Plot annotations and puzzle candidates need their own measured treatment.

How to apply the scale

The shared stylesheet supplies defaults. Consumer styles may still override them: repair the declaration that owns the layout and keep chart or board geometry local. See the design system for ownership and exceptions.

Transitions

A change of subject may use one ruled ornament. The surrounding sections then surrender their own boundary rules, so punctuation never accumulates into a stack.

Principles

  • Lead with the material, not a promotional masthead.
  • Make hierarchy skimmable through type, measure, rules, and disclosure.
  • Use margins for context and whitespace for separation before adding boxes.
  • Reserve color for identity, state, and comparison.
  • Keep every interaction legible with a keyboard, on a phone, and in print.
